Measure boundary

FY2023 · U3284001 ↗

Research space is net assignable square feet in buildings where science and engineering research occurs. It is a capacity measure, not building gross square footage, expenditures, or the whole campus footprint. Facilities is biennial. Blank years do not indicate zero, and the field categories reflect the survey rather than the university's named departments.
